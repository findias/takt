Cheat sheet

What you need on day one. Fits on one A4 sheet.

The interface is in Russian: labels below are quoted the way they appear on screen, with a translation next to them.

Where things are

WhereWhat is there
«Доски» (Boards)boards of the organisation and the archive
«Команда» (Team)people, invitations, labels, keys, subscriptions, export
«Структура» (Structure)subdivisions as a tree
Board header«Доска», «Таблица», «Изменения» — three views of one data set; «Поток» — metrics; «Архив» — archived cards

Keys

KeysAction
Ctrl+Kfind a card or a command
Arrowsmove between cards
Ctrl + arrowsmove a card between columns
Enteropen a card
Erename without opening
Escapeclose a panel, a menu or the palette

Five actions

  1. Create a board — on the main screen: name, key, «Завести».
  2. Create a card — at the bottom of a column, then Enter.
  3. Move a card — drag it, or press Ctrl with an arrow.
  4. Filter — the row above the board; the filter lives in the address bar, and «Сохранить вид» saves it as a named view.
  5. Archive — the «…» menu on a card, then «В архив»; «Вернуть» appears next to the message.

When something looks wrong

You seeIt means
A banner «Связи с сервером нет»the server is unreachable; nothing you did is lost
A card jumped backthe move never reached the server; press «Повторить»
The board shows less than you expecta filter is on; the column says how many are hidden
